About Michael

Designer by practice.
Technologist by training.

I’m a product designer at PTC with a Computer Science foundation. I work where technical systems, operational reality, and human judgment meet.

The throughline / 01

I make complicated systems feel understandable without hiding how they work.

My Computer Science background taught me to think in systems: dependencies, states, rules, and the consequences of changing one part. Product design gave me a way to apply that thinking to people’s actual work.

Today I specialize in identity and access management: authentication, SSO, OAuth, permissions, provisioning, and auditing. They’re technical domains, but the design challenge is deeply human: helping someone understand what they can do, what will happen next, and what could go wrong.

What guides the work / 02

Three beliefs I return to.

01

Complexity is information

The goal isn’t to remove every detail. It’s to reveal the right detail at the moment it becomes useful.

02

Systems should explain themselves

Good structure, language, and state design reduce the institutional knowledge a user needs.

03

Design gets better near the code

I work closely with engineers because technical constraints are part of the material.
